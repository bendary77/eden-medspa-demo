## Inspiration: what was learned, not copied

[Allure Aesthetics](https://allurefl.com/) uses warm neutral surfaces, generous margins, a large Cormorant Garamond headline, Jost supporting type, tracked small labels, restrained rectangular buttons and strong clinic imagery. Its inspected stylesheet uses a 1240px content width, roughly 78px major section padding, an 86px desktop hero title, and 42–54px section titles. These proportions make the information feel ordered and unhurried.

Eden borrows that restraint, not Allure’s section sequence or centered video-hero composition. The demo instead has an offset ivory editorial panel, a portrait-led first impression, a small storefront vignette and a compact grouped treatment directory. No Allure assets or copy were reused.
