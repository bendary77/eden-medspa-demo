# Eden Med Spa

Work-client homepage redesign pitch for Eden Med Spa in Boca Raton, owned by Limor Serri, MMS, PA-C. A static, Elementor-rebuildable concept with real brand assets and source-verified business information. This is a work project, not a personal project; it intentionally lives under `work/websites/` as requested.

## Current State

The demo is complete in `site/`, with local WebP images and fonts, responsive layouts, real booking/service links, Google Map, Boca Raton metadata and structured data. Two visual revision rounds are complete. Four viewport checks pass; Lighthouse performance/accessibility scores are 95/100 mobile and 100/100 desktop. Full-page screenshots, source register, client-facing notes and drop-ready ZIP are included. No public deployment or contact with the owner was made.

## Next

- Review the visual concept with the client; the production Elementor rebuild has not started.
- Confirm opening hours and the current treatment menu with Limor; source discrepancies are recorded in the research register.
- After approval, rebuild the broader page system in Elementor, remove demo-only noindex/footer wording and verify production hosting, accessibility and medical copy.
